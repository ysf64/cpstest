# 🖱️ CPS Test — Clicks Per Second

A simple, interactive **CPS (Clicks Per Second) Test** that measures how fast you can click your mouse. Challenge yourself, beat your personal records, and test your clicking speed!

## ✨ Features

- **Multiple Time Modes** — Choose between 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, or 100 seconds.
- **Live Timer** — Keep track of the remaining time during your test.
- **Real-Time Score** — See how many clicks you've made.
- **CPS Counter** — Instantly calculate your clicks per second.
- **Clean UI** — Modern dark-themed interface with colorful gradients and glowing elements.
- **Minecraft-Oriented** — Perfect for testing clicking speed for competitive Minecraft PvP.

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open the CPS Test.
2. Select your preferred test duration.
3. Click the designated area to start.
4. Click as fast as you can until the timer runs out.
5. Check your final score and CPS!

## 📊 What is CPS?

CPS stands for **Clicks Per Second**. It measures how many times you can click your mouse in one second.

The CPS score is calculated using:

`CPS = Total Clicks / Time (Seconds)`

For example, if you make 80 clicks in 10 seconds:

`80 / 10 = 8 CPS`

A higher CPS means faster clicking!

## 🎯 Why Use a CPS Test?

Clicking speed can play an important role in competitive Minecraft gameplay, especially in PvP.

Testing your CPS can help you:
- Measure your clicking speed.
- Track your improvement.
- Compare different clicking techniques.
- Challenge yourself to achieve a higher CPS.
